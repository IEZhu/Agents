## Answer A
**On-call checklist: API deployment (prod)**

- [ ] **Check the deployment status:**
  `kubectl rollout status deployment/api -n prod`
- [ ] **If 5xx errors more than doubled within 10 minutes after a release, roll back:**
  `kubectl rollout undo deployment/api -n prod`
- [ ] **Post in the #incidents channel** what was rolled back and at what time.
- [ ] **Do not restart the database** unless the on-call DBA has agreed to it.
- [ ] **Once things are stable, open a ticket** for the incident review.
