Here's a friendlier version that still gives the same answer:

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for getting in touch, and I'm sorry this isn't the answer you were hoping for. Our refund policy covers returns within 30 days of purchase. Your order is past that window, so unfortunately we can't offer a refund this time.
>
> You can find the full details in our terms here: [link]. If there's anything else we can help with, like a question about your order or getting the most out of your purchase, just reply to this email and we'll be glad to help.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your name]

A few notes on the changes:

- It still says no clearly. "Unfortunately we can't offer a refund" leaves nothing open to negotiation, so the customer won't take it as a maybe.
- I changed "Read the terms" to a link with a short pointer. The original sounds like you're telling them off, and the link gives them the same information without that tone.
- The offer of other help ends the email on a positive note without promising anything. If you have a real option to offer, such as store credit, an exchange or a repair, mention it there by name. If you don't, keep that line general so it doesn't suggest an exception you can't make.
