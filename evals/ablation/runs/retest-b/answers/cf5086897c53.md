I checked the other orders. Every one either misses a closing time or gets you home between 12:05 and 12:10, so this is the only plan that works.

### Why "nearest first" keeps failing
- The closest stop to home is the pharmacy (10 min). From there the nearest stop is the post office, which isn't open until 10:30. By the time you get to the dry cleaner, it has closed.
- The dry cleaner is the tightest deadline (10:00) and the farthest from everything else. It has to come first, even though it's a 20-minute drive.
- The post office opens late, so it belongs at the end of the route. It also sits near home.

### The catch: you have zero slack
The plan ends exactly at 12:00. A long line at the pharmacy or one red light too many will make you late. My suggestions:
- **Leave a few minutes early** if you can. Every minute before 9:00 is a minute of buffer.
- **If you're running late, drop the post office.** It's the only stop you can skip without breaking the rest of the plan. From the market (done by 11:10), drive straight home (12 min) and you're back around **11:22**, with over half an hour to spare. You also have until 2pm to go to the post office later, so you could stop there after pickup.
- **Checkpoint:** you should be leaving the hardware store by about 10:05. If you're past 10:10 there, plan on skipping the post office.
