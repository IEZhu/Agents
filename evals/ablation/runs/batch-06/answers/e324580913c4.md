1. **The evidence is thin.** Two customers on one call and "a bunch" of tickets is a hypothesis, not a validated problem. Pulling the ticket count is the most useful thing you can do before Friday. It sets how big the feature should be, and your VP will probably ask for it.
2. **Bulk currency change is risky, and I'd take it out of v1.** In an accounting app, changing an invoice's currency isn't a cosmetic edit. Either the amounts stay the same and now mean something different (100 USD becomes 100 EUR), or you have to decide on an FX conversion policy. Once an invoice has been issued, it's usually a document the customer has already received, and it may be a tax record. Editing that across 500 invoices in one click is how you end up with support escalations and bad books. Bulk "mark as paid" has the same kind of risk if it skips payment recording and reconciliation.

## PRD: Bulk Invoice Actions (v1)

**Author:** [you] · **Status:** Draft · **Eng review by:** [date] · **Target handoff:** Friday [date]

### 1. Problem

Users who manage many invoices have to open and edit them one at a time. This is slow and tedious, and it's easy to make mistakes when the same change (usually a due date or status) applies to dozens of invoices.

**Evidence so far:**
- 2 larger customers raised it on a call in [month]. Get the account names and their invoice volume per month.
- The support lead reports "a bunch" of tickets about editing invoices one by one. **Count: [TBD]** over the last [90] days. Include how many distinct accounts they came from.
- **Still needed:** how many of our ~18k accounts actually have enough invoices for this to matter. For example, the share of accounts with 50+ open invoices is **[TBD]**.

**Job to be done:** When I need to apply the same change to many invoices (for example, extending terms for a client or cleaning up old drafts), I want to do it in one action, so that I don't spend an hour clicking and don't miss any.

**Open question for discovery:** Which edits do people actually repeat? Tag the tickets by field (due date, status, currency, other). If 80% are due dates, v1 gets much smaller.

### 2. Proposed solution (high level)

- Add row checkboxes and a "select all" header checkbox to the invoice list.
- When one or more invoices are selected, show a **"Bulk actions"** menu above the table, plus a count ("12 selected").
- v1 actions: **Change due date** and **Change status** (limited set of transitions, see Scope).
- Every bulk action goes through a **confirmation step** that shows how many invoices will change, and which ones will be skipped and why.
- Show a **per-invoice result summary** afterwards (succeeded, skipped, failed).

### 4. Scope

**In scope (v1):**
- Multi-select on the invoice list, including "select all on this page".
- Bulk **change due date**: set a specific date, or optionally shift by N days (confirm with design whether shift is needed).
- Bulk **change status**, limited to safe transitions only. For example: Draft → Void/Deleted, and Sent → Void. **Exact list: [TBD with eng/finance]**.
- Validation per invoice. Invoices that aren't eligible are skipped and reported, not silently changed.
- Audit log entry per invoice changed (who, when, old value, new value, bulk-action ID).
- Permission check: only roles that can already edit invoices can use bulk actions.

**Out of scope (v1), deliberately:**
- **Bulk currency change.** Revisit only if ticket data shows real demand, and only for Draft invoices with an explicit FX policy.
- **Bulk "Mark as paid".** Payments should go through payment recording so reconciliation and ledger entries stay correct. Consider a proper "bulk record payment" flow later.
- Editing line items, amounts, tax, or customer on multiple invoices.
- "Select all N invoices matching this filter" across pages. Candidate for v2, see Milestones.
- Bulk undo (the audit log makes a manual or support-assisted revert possible in v1).
- Bulk sending or re-sending emails.

### 5. User stories

1. **Select invoices.** As a bookkeeper, I want to select several invoices from the list, so that I can act on them together.
   - AC: Given the invoice list, When I tick checkboxes, Then a selected count and the Bulk actions menu appear.
   - AC: Given invoices are selected, When I change page, filter, or sort, Then [TBD: selection is kept or cleared, decide and state it clearly in the UI].
   - AC: Given more than the maximum selection limit, When I try to select more, Then I see a message explaining the limit.

2. **Change due date.** As a bookkeeper, I want to set a new due date on selected invoices, so that I can extend terms for a client in one go.
   - AC: Given 20 selected invoices where 3 are Paid or Void, When I set a new due date and confirm, Then 17 are updated, and the summary lists the 3 skipped invoices with the reason.
   - AC: Given a due date is changed on a Sent invoice, Then scheduled payment reminders are recalculated from the new date. **[Confirm how reminders currently work.]**
   - AC: Given the new due date is earlier than the issue date, Then the action is blocked for those invoices, with a reason.

3. **Change status.** As an account owner, I want to void or delete several draft or stale invoices at once, so that I can clean up my list.
   - AC: Given selected invoices, When I choose a status, Then only allowed transitions are applied, and the others are skipped with a reason.
   - AC: Given a destructive transition (Void/Delete), Then the confirmation requires an explicit confirm step showing the count.

4. **Traceability.** As an account owner, I want to see who changed invoices in bulk, so that I can audit and correct mistakes.
   - AC: Given a bulk action completed, Then each changed invoice's history shows the change, the user, the timestamp, and a shared bulk-action ID.

5. **Partial failure.** As a user, I want to know exactly what did and didn't change, so that I'm not left with inconsistent data.
   - AC: Given some invoices fail (validation, concurrency, or a server error), Then the others still apply, and the result summary lists each failure with a reason, plus an option to retry the failed ones.

### 6. Technical considerations, risks and open questions

| Risk / question | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation / owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Large batches time out or lock tables | Medium | High | Eng to decide sync vs. async (a background job with progress for large batches). Set the limit based on load testing, not a guess. |
| Where "500" came from is unclear | — | Medium | Confirm with the 2 customers what their real batch size is. Default page size may make 500 moot unless we do cross-page select. |
| Status changes break accounting (ledger, reconciliation, tax reports) | Medium | High | Limit transitions in v1. Finance or accounting SME to review the list. |
| Editing Sent invoices changes documents customers already have | Medium | Medium | Decide whether a due-date change on Sent invoices notifies the customer. Default: no auto-email in v1. |
| Integrations and webhooks (for example, synced accounting or payment tools) get flooded with 500 update events | [TBD] | Medium | Eng to check outbound sync and webhook behavior and rate limits. |
| Concurrent edits (someone edits an invoice mid-batch) | Low | Medium | Per-invoice optimistic locking. Skip conflicting invoices and report them. |
| Users make a large mistake | Medium | High | Confirmation step, audit log. Consider bulk undo in v2 if the revert rate is high. |

**Assumptions to verify with eng before committing to dates:**
- There's an existing per-invoice edit endpoint with validation we can reuse, rather than a new bulk write path that skips the rules.
- An audit or history log exists for invoices. If not, that's added scope.
- Payment reminders are computed from the due date at send time, not stored per invoice. This affects story 2.
