## Answer A
Below is a spec draft you can hand to engineering. Before you send it, there are two problems with the current write-up that I'd fix, because engineering will find them anyway:

1. **The evidence is thin.** Two customers on one call and "a bunch" of tickets is a hypothesis, not a validated problem. Pulling the ticket count is the most useful thing you can do before Friday. It sets how big the feature should be, and your VP will probably ask for it.
2. **Bulk currency change is risky, and I'd take it out of v1.** In an accounting app, changing an invoice's currency isn't a cosmetic edit. Either the amounts stay the same and now mean something different (100 USD becomes 100 EUR), or you have to decide on an FX conversion policy. Once an invoice has been issued, it's usually a document the customer has already received, and it may be a tax record. Editing that across 500 invoices in one click is how you end up with support escalations and bad books. Bulk "mark as paid" has the same kind of risk if it skips payment recording and reconciliation.

I've written the spec around those points. Fill in the `[TBD]` fields. I haven't made up any numbers.

**Author:** [you] · **Status:** Draft · **Eng review by:** [date] · **Target handoff:** Friday [date]

- Show a **per-invoice result summary** afterwards (succeeded, skipped, failed).

### 4. Scope

**In scope (v1):**
- Multi-select on the invoice list, including "select all on this page".
- Bulk **change due date**: set a specific date, or optionally shift by N days (confirm with design whether shift is needed).
- Bulk **change status**, limited to safe transitions only. For example: Draft → Void/Deleted, and Sent → Void. **Exact list: [TBD with eng/finance]**.

- Audit log entry per invoice changed (who, when, old value, new value, bulk-action ID).
- Permission check: only roles that can already edit invoices can use bulk actions.

**Out of scope (v1), deliberately:**
- **Bulk currency change.** Revisit only if ticket data shows real demand, and only for Draft invoices with an explicit FX policy.
- **Bulk "Mark as paid".** Payments should go through payment recording so reconciliation and ledger entries stay correct. Consider a proper "bulk record payment" flow later.
- Editing line items, amounts, tax, or customer on multiple invoices.
- "Select all N invoices matching this filter" across pages. Candidate for v2, see Milestones.
- Bulk undo (the audit log makes a manual or support-assisted revert possible in v1).
- Bulk sending or re-sending emails.

2. **Change due date.** As a bookkeeper, I want to set a new due date on selected invoices, so that I can extend terms for a client in one go.

   - AC: Given a due date is changed on a Sent invoice, Then scheduled payment reminders are recalculated from the new date. **[Confirm how reminders currently work.]**

3. **Change status.** As an account owner, I want to void or delete several draft or stale invoices at once, so that I can clean up my list.

4. **Traceability.** As an account owner, I want to see who changed invoices in bulk, so that I can audit and correct mistakes.

5. **Partial failure.** As a user, I want to know exactly what did and didn't change, so that I'm not left with inconsistent data.

## Answer B
The full spec is below, ready to hand off. Before you send it, fix two things in your draft. Engineering and your VP will likely push back on both.

1. **The evidence is thin.** Right now the spec rests on two enterprise-leaning customers and "a bunch" of tickets. With about 18k accounts, that could be a real pattern or a loud minority. You can get better numbers before Friday (see "Before Friday" at the end). I've left the figures as `[TBD]` placeholders and made none up.
2. **The three edits carry very different levels of risk.** Changing due dates in bulk is low risk. Changing **currency** in bulk is probably the most dangerous thing in the draft. On an invoice that has already been issued, a currency change either leaves the amounts unconverted (so the invoice is wrong) or converts them (which raises exchange-rate, tax and ledger questions). Bulk **status** changes, such as marking invoices Paid, can create ledger entries with no payment behind them. I've scoped the MVP to match. If you disagree, argue it with your VP and eng lead, but don't let it slip through unexamined.

## PRD: Bulk Invoice Editing (MVP)

### Problem
Users who manage many invoices have to open and edit each one separately to make the same change to all of them. The typical case is pushing back due dates for a batch of clients. This takes a lot of time and invites mistakes.

**Evidence (to be completed before handoff):**
- Support tickets about editing multiple invoices, last 6 months: `[TBD: count, % of total tickets, how many accounts]`
- Customer calls: 2 customers on [date] asked for bulk editing. Their segment and invoice volume: `[TBD]`
- Behavioral signal: accounts that edit 10 or more invoices in one session, and how often: `[TBD: product analytics]`
- Which field users edit in sequential sessions: `[TBD]`. This tells us whether due date really is the main job.

**Job to be done:** When I need to apply the same change to many invoices (new payment terms, a correction, a status clean-up), I want to do it in one action, so that I don't spend an hour clicking or introduce inconsistencies.

### Solution
Add multi-select to the invoice list, plus a "Bulk actions" menu that applies one change to all selected invoices. The system checks each invoice for eligibility, shows a preview and asks for confirmation, applies the change, and reports results per invoice, including any it skipped and why. Every change is written to the audit log.

| Median time to update due dates on N invoices | `[TBD: measure the current flow]` | `[TBD]` | Launch |
| Bulk actions reverted or followed by a support ticket (guardrail) | n/a | < `[TBD]`% | Ongoing |

The guardrail metric matters as much as adoption. A bulk action that corrupts data is worse than having no bulk action.

### Scope
**In scope (MVP):**
- Checkbox selection on the invoice list: per row, and "select all on this page"
- "Bulk actions" dropdown above the table, active only when at least one invoice is selected
- **Change due date** (set a specific date, or shift by +/- N days)
- **Change status**, limited to transitions that don't touch the ledger: `[confirm with eng/finance, e.g. Draft to Void, Mark as Sent]`
- Pre-flight eligibility check, confirmation dialog, per-invoice result summary
- Audit log entry for each affected invoice (user, timestamp, old and new values, bulk operation ID)
- Selection limit: `[500, or a validated number]`

**Out of scope (explicitly):**
- **Bulk currency change.** Deferred pending a separate decision (see Risks). If it's added later, it should probably apply to Drafts only.
- Bulk "Mark as Paid" and any other transition that creates payment or ledger entries
- Editing line items, amounts, tax, or customers in bulk
- "Select all N invoices matching this filter" across pages (candidate for v2)
- Bulk undo (v2; the audit log is the MVP safety net)
- Bulk send or re-send of invoices and reminders
- Public API endpoints for bulk editing

### User Stories

1. **As a** bookkeeper, **I want** to select several invoices from the list, **so that** I can act on them together.
   - AC: Given the invoice list, When I tick row checkboxes, Then a counter shows "N selected" and the Bulk actions menu becomes active.
   - AC: Given I tick "select all" in the header, When the page has 50 rows, Then all 50 on the current page are selected and the counter says so. It does not silently select invoices on other pages.
   - AC: Given I have selected invoices, When I change the filter or sort, Then `[selection clears / persists: decide]` and the UI says which happened.

2. **As a** bookkeeper, **I want** to change the due date on many invoices at once, **so that** I can apply new payment terms quickly.
   - AC: Given N selected invoices, When I choose "Change due date" and enter a date, Then I see a preview: "X will be updated, Y skipped (reason)".
   - AC: Given an invoice that is Paid or Void, When the bulk action runs, Then it is skipped and listed with its reason.
   - AC: Given an invoice falls in a locked or closed accounting period, When the action runs, Then it is skipped with the reason "period locked".
   - AC: Given updated invoices have scheduled payment reminders, When the due date changes, Then reminders are rescheduled to match the new date `[confirm the intended behavior]`.
   - AC: Given the action completes, Then each change appears in the invoice audit history.

3. **As a** bookkeeper, **I want** to change the status of many invoices at once, **so that** I can clean up my invoice list.
   - AC: Given selected invoices, When I choose "Change status", Then only the allowed target statuses are offered.
   - AC: Given an invoice whose current status can't move to the target, When the action runs, Then it is skipped with its reason and the rest proceed.

4. **As a** user without edit permissions, **I want** the bulk actions to be unavailable, **so that** I can't change invoices I'm not allowed to change.
   - AC: Given my role cannot edit invoices, When I view the list, Then checkboxes or bulk actions are hidden or disabled.

5. **As a** user running a large bulk action, **I want** clear progress and results, **so that** I know exactly what changed.
   - AC: Given more than `[threshold]` invoices, When I confirm, Then the action runs in the background, and I can leave the page and am notified when it finishes.
   - AC: Given some invoices fail, When the job finishes, Then I see which ones failed and why, and nothing is left half-updated (the edit to each invoice is atomic).
