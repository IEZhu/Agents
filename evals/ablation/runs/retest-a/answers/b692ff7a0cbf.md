**Stop before filing. My previous answer was wrong on the court.** This dispute goes to the **Arbitrazh Court of the City of Moscow** (Арбитражный суд города Москвы), not a district court. And **you most likely cannot file tomorrow**: unless a written pre-trial claim (претензия) went out at least 30 days ago, that has to happen first.

## 1. Why arbitrazh and not a district court

- Your client is registered as an ИП and the dispute comes out of her business (a commercial supply). Under **Art. 27(1)–(2) APK RF**, economic disputes between legal entities and individual entrepreneurs belong to arbitrazh courts. Being a natural person doesn't matter here. What matters is her status as an entrepreneur and the commercial nature of the dispute.
- **Territorial jurisdiction** is the defendant's location (**Art. 35 APK RF**). The contract has no jurisdiction clause, so that's Moscow, based on the OOO's registered address in ЕГРЮЛ.
- If you file with a district court, it will either return the claim or send it to the arbitrazh court (**Art. 33.1 GPK RF**). Either way you lose weeks.

The substantive part of my earlier answer still stands: you claim the debt under **Arts. 486 and 516 GK RF** plus interest under **Art. 395 GK RF**. Check one thing first. If the contract has a contractual penalty (неустойка) for late payment, **Art. 395(4) GK RF** says you claim the penalty, not Art. 395 interest.

## 2. Why not tomorrow: the mandatory pre-trial claim

**Art. 4(5) APK RF**: in civil-law disputes over money owed, you may go to the arbitrazh court only **30 calendar days after the претензия is sent**. The contract can set a different period or procedure, but yours says nothing, so the statutory 30 days apply.

- **If a претензия was already sent 30+ days ago** (demanding payment of the debt, with proof of dispatch), you can file as soon as the package is ready.
- **If not**, send one today to the address in ЕГРЮЛ by registered mail with a list of contents (опись вложения), or by any method the contract allows. You can file 30 days later. If you file without it, the court will leave the claim without consideration or return it (**Art. 128 / Art. 148(1)(2) APK RF**).

## 3. What to attach (Art. 125–126 APK RF)

1. **Proof of state duty paid** (see the amount below).
2. **Proof you sent the defendant a copy of the statement of claim** with attachments it doesn't already have (post receipt plus опись). **Art. 125(3) APK RF** requires this *before* filing.
3. **Proof of pre-trial compliance**: the претензия, proof of dispatch, and any reply.
4. **Documents supporting the claim**:
   - the supply contract and any specifications or supplementary agreements
   - delivery documents (УПД / ТОРГ-12 / waybills) signed by the buyer
   - invoices
   - a reconciliation statement (акт сверки), if you have one. A signed one is also useful against a limitation-period argument.
   - any correspondence acknowledging the debt
5. **The amount claimed**: a calculation of the principal plus the Art. 395 interest (or contractual penalty), signed by the claimant or representative. State the interest both as a sum to date and as continuing until actual payment (**Art. 395(3) GK RF**; Plenum of the Supreme Court Resolution No. 7 of 24.03.2016, para. 48).
6. **Registration extracts**:
   - an ЕГРИП extract for your client
   - an ЕГРЮЛ extract for the defendant, dated no earlier than 30 days before filing. The electronic extract from nalog.ru is accepted.
7. **Power of attorney** (or other authority) for the person signing, if that isn't your client. Since 2017 a representative in arbitrazh courts needs a law degree or an advocate's status (**Art. 59(3) APK RF**), so attach a copy of your diploma or advocate's certificate.

**Filing**: the easiest route is electronic filing through **«Мой арбитр»** (my.arbitr.ru), using a Госуслуги account or a qualified e-signature. Scanned attachments are fine.

## 4. State duty

After the 2024 amendments to **Art. 333.21 NK RF** (Federal Law No. 176-FZ), a claim between 1,000,001 and 10,000,000 RUB costs **55,000 RUB + 3% of the amount over 1,000,000 RUB**. On 1.2M principal alone that comes to about **61,000 RUB**, and it goes up slightly once you add the interest. (I'm recalling these figures, not verifying them here. Check the current text of Art. 333.21 before you pay.) The court will charge the duty to the defendant if you win (**Art. 110 APK RF**).

## 5. Procedure note

At 1.2M+ the claim is above the limits for a court order (судебный приказ, **Art. 229.2 APK RF**) and for simplified proceedings for an ИП claimant (**Art. 227 APK RF**). Both thresholds were raised in recent years, so confirm the current numbers, but 1.2M clearly exceeds both. Expect ordinary action proceedings (исковое производство).

## Risks

- If you file before the 30 days run out, or without proof of the претензия, the claim will be returned or left without consideration.
- Missing proof that the defendant was sent the claim leads to the claim being left without movement (**Art. 128 APK RF**).
- If the contract actually has a penalty clause or a payment-deferral clause, your calculation changes. Re-read it before drafting.
