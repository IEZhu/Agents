## 1. Wrong court: this goes to the arbitrazh court, not a district court

Your client is an individual entrepreneur (ИП). She is suing an ООО over a supply contract she entered into as part of her business. That makes it an economic dispute between business entities, and those go to the **arbitrazh courts**, not courts of general jurisdiction:

- **АПК РФ, Art. 27(1)–(2) and Art. 28**: economic disputes involving legal entities and individual entrepreneurs, arising from business activity, fall under arbitrazh court jurisdiction.
- A supply contract (договор поставки, **ГК РФ Art. 506**) is by definition a business contract. It concerns goods for use in the buyer's business.

**Venue**: with no dispute clause in the contract, the general rule applies and the claim is filed where the defendant is located (**АПК РФ Art. 35**). For a Moscow ООО that is the **Арбитражный суд города Москвы**. Check the defendant's registered address in its current ЕГРЮЛ extract.

If you file with a district court, it will most likely return the claim for lack of jurisdiction (ГПК РФ Art. 135) or transfer it (ГПК РФ Art. 33). Either way you lose weeks. Please redraft the claim under the АПК rather than the ГПК.

## 2. Filing tomorrow depends on the pre-trial claim (претензия)

In arbitrazh courts the pre-trial claim procedure is **mandatory** for monetary claims arising from contracts (**АПК РФ Art. 4(5)**). The rules are:

- The creditor must send a written claim (претензия) to the debtor first.
- The creditor may sue only after **30 calendar days from the date the претензия was sent**, unless the contract or a statute sets another period. Your contract is silent, so the 30 days apply.

What this means for filing tomorrow:
- **She has already sent a претензия, and 30 or more days have passed since sending**: yes, you can file tomorrow.
- **No претензия has been sent, or it was sent less than 30 days ago**: no. The court will return the claim under **АПК РФ Art. 129(1)(5)**. Send the претензия now, by registered mail with a list of contents and a return receipt, or by another method you can prove. Also email it if the contract allows notices by email. Then wait out the 30 days.

The претензия should state the debt, the contract and delivery documents it rests on, a payment deadline, and that she will claim interest under ГК РФ Art. 395 and costs.

## 3. What to attach (АПК РФ Art. 126)

1. **Proof that a copy of the claim and its attachments was sent to the defendant**: postal receipt and list of contents. The defendant must receive the full package before you file.
2. **Proof the state fee was paid**: a payment order with a bank stamp, or an electronic payment confirmation.
3. **Proof the pre-trial procedure was followed**: a copy of the претензия plus proof of sending (receipt, list of contents, tracking printout), and any reply from the defendant.
4. **Documents supporting the claim**: the supply contract, specifications, and delivery documents (ТОРГ-12 or УПД signed by the buyer). Add any reconciliation statement (акт сверки), invoices and correspondence acknowledging the debt.
5. **Calculation of the amount claimed**: principal plus Art. 395 interest, set out period by period against the Bank of Russia key rate. If the contract has a contractual penalty (неустойка), claim that instead. Art. 395(4) ГК РФ bars recovering both for the same breach unless the contract provides otherwise.
6. **Registry extracts**: an ЕГРИП extract for your client and an ЕГРЮЛ extract for the defendant. A printout from the ФНС website is fine. Under Art. 126(1)(9), each must be obtained no earlier than 30 days before filing.
7. **Representative's authority**: a power of attorney from the ИП, plus a copy of your law degree or advocate status (**АПК РФ Art. 59(3), Art. 61(3)**).
8. Optionally, a motion for interim measures (**Art. 90–91**) if you fear the defendant will move assets, and a motion for preliminary court hearing scheduling preferences.
