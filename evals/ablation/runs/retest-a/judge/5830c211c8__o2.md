## Answer A
Before you go further, I need to correct my previous answer. It was wrong on the choice of court, and there is a second problem with filing tomorrow.

## 1. Wrong court: this goes to the arbitrazh court, not a district court

Your client is an individual entrepreneur (ИП). She is suing an ООО over a supply contract she entered into as part of her business. That makes it an economic dispute between business entities, and those go to the **arbitrazh courts**, not courts of general jurisdiction:

- **АПК РФ, Art. 27(1)–(2) and Art. 28**: economic disputes involving legal entities and individual entrepreneurs, arising from business activity, fall under arbitrazh court jurisdiction.
- A supply contract (договор поставки, **ГК РФ Art. 506**) is by definition a business contract. It concerns goods for use in the buyer's business.

**Venue**: with no dispute clause in the contract, the general rule applies and the claim is filed where the defendant is located (**АПК РФ Art. 35**). For a Moscow ООО that is the **Арбитражный суд города Москвы**. Check the defendant's registered address in its current ЕГРЮЛ extract.

If you file with a district court, it will most likely return the claim for lack of jurisdiction (ГПК РФ Art. 135) or transfer it (ГПК РФ Art. 33). Either way you lose weeks. Please redraft the claim under the АПК rather than the ГПК.

## 2. Filing tomorrow depends on the pre-trial claim (претензия)

In arbitrazh courts the pre-trial claim procedure is **mandatory** for monetary claims arising from contracts (**АПК РФ Art. 4(5)**). The rules are:

- The creditor must send a written claim (претензия) to the debtor first.
- The creditor may sue only after **30 calendar days from the date the претензия was sent**, unless the contract or a statute sets another period. Your contract is silent, so the 30 days apply.

What this means for filing tomorrow:
- **She has already sent a претензия, and 30 or more days have passed since sending**: yes, you can file tomorrow.
- **No претензия has been sent, or it was sent less than 30 days ago**: no. The court will return the claim under **АПК РФ Art. 129(1)(5)**. Send the претензия now, by registered mail with a list of contents and a return receipt, or by another method you can prove. Also email it if the contract allows notices by email. Then wait out the 30 days.

The претензия should state the debt, the contract and delivery documents it rests on, a payment deadline, and that she will claim interest under ГК РФ Art. 395 and costs.

## 3. What to attach (АПК РФ Art. 126)

1. **Proof that a copy of the claim and its attachments was sent to the defendant**: postal receipt and list of contents. The defendant must receive the full package before you file.
2. **Proof the state fee was paid**: a payment order with a bank stamp, or an electronic payment confirmation.
3. **Proof the pre-trial procedure was followed**: a copy of the претензия plus proof of sending (receipt, list of contents, tracking printout), and any reply from the defendant.
4. **Documents supporting the claim**: the supply contract, specifications, and delivery documents (ТОРГ-12 or УПД signed by the buyer). Add any reconciliation statement (акт сверки), invoices and correspondence acknowledging the debt.
5. **Calculation of the amount claimed**: principal plus Art. 395 interest, set out period by period against the Bank of Russia key rate. If the contract has a contractual penalty (неустойка), claim that instead. Art. 395(4) ГК РФ bars recovering both for the same breach unless the contract provides otherwise.
6. **Registry extracts**: an ЕГРИП extract for your client and an ЕГРЮЛ extract for the defendant. A printout from the ФНС website is fine. Under Art. 126(1)(9), each must be obtained no earlier than 30 days before filing.
7. **Representative's authority**: a power of attorney from the ИП, plus a copy of your law degree or advocate status (**АПК РФ Art. 59(3), Art. 61(3)**).
8. Optionally, a motion for interim measures (**Art. 90–91**) if you fear the defendant will move assets, and a motion for preliminary court hearing scheduling preferences.

## 4. State fee

The amount is 1.2M RUB, which is above the simplified-procedure limit for claims brought by an ИП. As I recall, that limit is 400,000 RUB under **АПК РФ Art. 227(2)(1)** (recalled, not verified). The case will therefore go through ordinary proceedings. It is also above the court-order (судебный приказ) threshold under Art. 229.2, so a court order is not available either.

Under **НК РФ Art. 333.21** as amended from 8 September 2024, a claim of 1,000,001–2,000,000 RUB pays **55,000 RUB + 1% of the amount over 1,000,000 RUB**. On 1.2M that comes to about **57,000 RUB** (recalled, not verified; confirm the current scale in the source). The price of the claim includes the Art. 395 interest, so recompute the fee on the total you actually claim.

## 5. Practical notes

- **File electronically through "Мой арбитр"** (my.arbitr.ru), logging in with Госуслуги. This avoids travelling to Moscow. Scanned attachments are accepted, and the court can request originals later.
- **Costs**: claim the state fee and legal fees in the claim itself (**АПК РФ Art. 110, 112**).
- **Hearings**: the Moscow Arbitrazh Court usually allows online participation. File a motion for web-conference participation under **АПК РФ Art. 153.2**.

**Bottom line**: re-address the claim to the Арбитражный суд города Москвы under the АПК. You can file tomorrow only if a претензия was sent at least 30 days ago and the claim has already been dispatched to the defendant. Otherwise, send the претензия today and file once the 30 days have run.

## Answer B
**Stop before filing. My previous answer was wrong on the court.** This dispute goes to the **Arbitrazh Court of the City of Moscow** (Арбитражный суд города Москвы), not a district court. And **you most likely cannot file tomorrow**: unless a written pre-trial claim (претензия) went out at least 30 days ago, that has to happen first.

## 1. Why arbitrazh and not a district court

- Your client is registered as an ИП and the dispute comes out of her business (a commercial supply). Under **Art. 27(1)–(2) APK RF**, economic disputes between legal entities and individual entrepreneurs belong to arbitrazh courts. Being a natural person doesn't matter here. What matters is her status as an entrepreneur and the commercial nature of the dispute.
- **Territorial jurisdiction** is the defendant's location (**Art. 35 APK RF**). The contract has no jurisdiction clause, so that's Moscow, based on the OOO's registered address in ЕГРЮЛ.
- If you file with a district court, it will either return the claim or send it to the arbitrazh court (**Art. 33.1 GPK RF**). Either way you lose weeks.

The substantive part of my earlier answer still stands: you claim the debt under **Arts. 486 and 516 GK RF** plus interest under **Art. 395 GK RF**. Check one thing first. If the contract has a contractual penalty (неустойка) for late payment, **Art. 395(4) GK RF** says you claim the penalty, not Art. 395 interest.

## 2. Why not tomorrow: the mandatory pre-trial claim

**Art. 4(5) APK RF**: in civil-law disputes over money owed, you may go to the arbitrazh court only **30 calendar days after the претензия is sent**. The contract can set a different period or procedure, but yours says nothing, so the statutory 30 days apply.

- **If a претензия was already sent 30+ days ago** (demanding payment of the debt, with proof of dispatch), you can file as soon as the package is ready.
- **If not**, send one today to the address in ЕГРЮЛ by registered mail with a list of contents (опись вложения), or by any method the contract allows. You can file 30 days later. If you file without it, the court will leave the claim without consideration or return it (**Art. 128 / Art. 148(1)(2) APK RF**).

## 3. What to attach (Art. 125–126 APK RF)

1. **Proof of state duty paid** (see the amount below).
2. **Proof you sent the defendant a copy of the statement of claim** with attachments it doesn't already have (post receipt plus опись). **Art. 125(3) APK RF** requires this *before* filing.
3. **Proof of pre-trial compliance**: the претензия, proof of dispatch, and any reply.
4. **Documents supporting the claim**:
   - the supply contract and any specifications or supplementary agreements
   - delivery documents (УПД / ТОРГ-12 / waybills) signed by the buyer
   - invoices
   - a reconciliation statement (акт сверки), if you have one. A signed one is also useful against a limitation-period argument.
   - any correspondence acknowledging the debt
5. **The amount claimed**: a calculation of the principal plus the Art. 395 interest (or contractual penalty), signed by the claimant or representative. State the interest both as a sum to date and as continuing until actual payment (**Art. 395(3) GK RF**; Plenum of the Supreme Court Resolution No. 7 of 24.03.2016, para. 48).
6. **Registration extracts**:
   - an ЕГРИП extract for your client
   - an ЕГРЮЛ extract for the defendant, dated no earlier than 30 days before filing. The electronic extract from nalog.ru is accepted.
7. **Power of attorney** (or other authority) for the person signing, if that isn't your client. Since 2017 a representative in arbitrazh courts needs a law degree or an advocate's status (**Art. 59(3) APK RF**), so attach a copy of your diploma or advocate's certificate.

**Filing**: the easiest route is electronic filing through **«Мой арбитр»** (my.arbitr.ru), using a Госуслуги account or a qualified e-signature. Scanned attachments are fine.

## 4. State duty

After the 2024 amendments to **Art. 333.21 NK RF** (Federal Law No. 176-FZ), a claim between 1,000,001 and 10,000,000 RUB costs **55,000 RUB + 3% of the amount over 1,000,000 RUB**. On 1.2M principal alone that comes to about **61,000 RUB**, and it goes up slightly once you add the interest. (I'm recalling these figures, not verifying them here. Check the current text of Art. 333.21 before you pay.) The court will charge the duty to the defendant if you win (**Art. 110 APK RF**).

## 5. Procedure note

At 1.2M+ the claim is above the limits for a court order (судебный приказ, **Art. 229.2 APK RF**) and for simplified proceedings for an ИП claimant (**Art. 227 APK RF**). Both thresholds were raised in recent years, so confirm the current numbers, but 1.2M clearly exceeds both. Expect ordinary action proceedings (исковое производство).

## Risks

- If you file before the 30 days run out, or without proof of the претензия, the claim will be returned or left without consideration.
- Missing proof that the defendant was sent the claim leads to the claim being left without movement (**Art. 128 APK RF**).
- If the contract actually has a penalty clause or a payment-deferral clause, your calculation changes. Re-read it before drafting.
- The citations above are from recall. Check the current wording in КонсультантПлюс/Гарант before filing. This is information, not legal advice. Final decisions belong to the licensed lawyer handling the case.
