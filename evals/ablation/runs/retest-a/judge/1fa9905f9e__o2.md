## Latest user message
I'm a foreign engineer on a work permit in Russia and just bought a used 2019 Toyota RAV4, 249 hp, registered in my name. A colleague said Russian car tax is a flat amount per horsepower nationwide. How much will I owe per year and when do I pay it?

2. Asks for or conditions the answer on the specific region, rather than presenting one number as the national figure

## Answer A
**Short answer (Russia):** Your colleague is only partly right. Russian transport tax (транспортный налог) is charged per horsepower, but the rate is **not the same everywhere**. Each region sets its own rate, and the rate goes up with engine power. For a 249 hp car the yearly bill can be anywhere from a few thousand to about 19,000 rubles, depending on the region where the car is registered. In Moscow it is about **18,675 RUB a year**. You pay it **once a year, by 1 December of the following year**, after the tax office (ФНС) sends you a notice. Being a foreigner on a work permit doesn't change any of this.

## Why it isn't a flat national rate

- Transport tax is a **regional tax** under Chapter 28 of the Tax Code (НК РФ). Article 361 sets federal base rates, and regional laws can raise or lower them. Under Art. 361(2) (recalled, not verified), a region can change the base rate by **up to 10 times**.
- The rates come in **power bands**: up to 100 hp, 100–150, 150–200, 200–250, and over 250. It is **not a marginal scale** like income tax. The rate for your band is multiplied by **all** of the car's horsepower.
- Your 249 hp falls in the **"over 200 up to 250 hp"** band. That matters: at 251 hp you would move into a much more expensive band.

## Rough numbers for 249 hp

| Region | Rate for 200–250 hp band | Yearly tax |
|---|---|---|
| Federal base (Art. 361 НК РФ) | ~7.5 RUB/hp | ~1,868 RUB |
| Moscow (Moscow Law No. 33 of 09.07.2008) | 75 RUB/hp | **18,675 RUB** |
| St. Petersburg | ~75 RUB/hp | ~18,675 RUB |
| Many other regions | often 45–75 RUB/hp | ~11,000–18,700 RUB |

I recalled these rates and did not check them for the current tax year. Some rates were changed in 2024–2025, so confirm yours before relying on it. The official source is the FNS calculator and rate lookup at **nalog.gov.ru** ("Справочная информация о ставках и льготах по имущественным налогам"). Pick the region where the car is registered and the year.

**Formula:** tax = horsepower × regional rate × (months you owned the car ÷ 12) × luxury coefficient (if it applies)

- **First year is prorated.** If the car was registered to you **on or before the 15th** of a month, that month counts as a full month. If it was registered after the 15th, that month doesn't count. For example, a car registered on 10 March counts 10 months, so in Moscow the tax is about 18,675 × 10/12 ≈ 15,563 RUB. The seller pays for the months before the sale in the same way (Art. 362(3) НК РФ, recalled).
- **Luxury coefficient:** this does not apply to you. It only covers cars on the Ministry of Industry and Trade's list of cars with an average price of 10 million RUB or more. A 2019 RAV4 is well below that.

## How and when you pay

1. **You don't file anything.** The FNS calculates the tax from traffic police (ГИБДД) registration data (Art. 362(1) НК РФ).
2. **Tax notice (налоговое уведомление):** it arrives in autumn of the following year, either in your личный кабинет налогоплательщика on nalog.gov.ru or by post. Tax for 2026 will be billed in 2027.
3. **Deadline: 1 December of the year after the tax year** (Art. 363(1) НК РФ). You pay through the single tax payment (единый налоговый платёж, ЕНП). Late payment adds penalty interest (пени).
4. **If no notice arrives**, you must tell the FNS yourself that you own a taxable vehicle. The deadline is 31 December of the year after the tax year (Art. 23(2.1) НК РФ). If you don't, you can be fined 20% of the unpaid tax. To avoid this, get an **ИНН** and open your личный кабинет early. Foreign nationals can do this at any FNS office with their passport and registration.

## Risks and caveats

- **Region matters most.** The tax depends on where the car is **registered**, which is usually your place of registration (регистрация). Moving the registration to another region changes the rate.
- **Rates change.** Regional laws are often amended. Check the rate for the exact tax year, not an older figure.
- **Check your notice.** Make sure the horsepower in the notice matches your ПТС/ЭПТС (249 hp). Errors happen and can be challenged with the FNS.
- **Regional exemptions (льготы)** exist, for example for certain disabilities, large families or electric cars. Most won't apply to you, but you can see them with the same FNS rate lookup.

This is general information, not legal advice. For anything binding, check with a Russian tax adviser or your local FNS office.


## Answer B
**Short answer:** Your colleague is wrong. Russian transport tax (транспортный налог) is a **regional** tax. The Tax Code sets base rates per horsepower, and each region (субъект РФ) sets its own rate within limits. How much you owe therefore depends on the region where the car is registered. For a 249 hp car the yearly figure can be anywhere from about 1,900 ₽ to about 19,000 ₽. You don't file anything. The tax office (ФНС) sends you a notice, and you pay **by 1 December of the following year** (tax for 2025 is due by 1 December 2026).

## Why it isn't a flat national rate

- **Tax Code of the Russian Federation (НК РФ), Chapter 28.** Transport tax is listed as a regional tax. The Code sets the framework, and regional laws set the actual rates (art. 356).
- **Art. 361(1)** sets base rates in rubles per horsepower, graded by engine power. **Art. 361(2)** lets regions raise or lower those rates, up to 10 times. That's why the same car costs very different amounts in, say, Moscow and a region with low rates.
- **Your status as a foreigner doesn't matter.** The taxpayer is the person the vehicle is registered to (art. 357), whatever their citizenship or tax residency.

## Rough numbers for 249 hp

These rates are from memory and I haven't checked them here. Confirm them for your region before you rely on them.

| Region | Rate for the 225/200–250 hp band | Yearly tax (249 hp) |
|---|---|---|
| Moscow | ~75 ₽/hp | ~18,675 ₽ |
| St Petersburg | ~75 ₽/hp | ~18,675 ₽ |
| Old federal base rate (200–250 hp) | 7.5 ₽/hp | ~1,868 ₽ |

**A 2025 change you should know about:** the 2024 tax reform rewrote art. 361 with effect from 2025. As I recall, it added more power brackets and raised the base rates, and many regions changed their own rates at the same time. The federal row above may therefore be out of date. Moscow's and St Petersburg's rates have been stable for years, but check them too.

**Formula:** tax = hp × regional rate × (months you owned the car ÷ 12) × luxury multiplier (if any).

- **First year is pro-rated (art. 362(3)).** If the car was registered to you on or before the 15th of a month, that month counts. If it was registered after the 15th, the month doesn't count. The seller pays for the months before the transfer.
- **Luxury multiplier (art. 362(2)).** This only applies to cars on the Ministry of Industry and Trade's list of cars costing more than 10 million ₽ (the threshold was raised from 3 million ₽). A 2019 RAV4 isn't on that list, so no multiplier.

## How and when you pay

1. The ФНС calculates the tax and sends you a tax notice (налоговое уведомление), usually in autumn. It arrives through your personal taxpayer account (Личный кабинет налогоплательщика) if you've set one up, and by post if not.
2. You pay by **1 December** of the year after the tax year (art. 363(1)). This goes through the single tax payment (единый налоговый платёж). If you pay late, penalty interest (пени) accrues.
3. **If no notice arrives:** you still have to tell the ФНС that you own a taxable vehicle, by 31 December of the year after the tax year (НК РФ art. 23(2.1)). Otherwise you can be fined 20% of the unpaid tax. Setting up a Личный кабинет with your ИНН is the easiest way to avoid this.

## How to get your exact figure

Use the ФНС "Tax calculator: transport tax" (Налоговый калькулятор – транспортный налог) on nalog.gov.ru. You pick your region, the year, the engine power and how many months you owned the car, and it applies the current regional rate.

*This is general information, not legal advice. Check the rates and your region's current law, or ask a licensed tax adviser in Russia.*
