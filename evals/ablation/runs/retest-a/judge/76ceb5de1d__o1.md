## Answer A
**Short answer:** Both assumptions are out of date for Russia in 2026. Profit tax is **25%**, not 20%, and standard VAT is **22%**, not 20%. The model also leaves out social insurance contributions, dividend withholding tax to a Dutch parent (no treaty relief applies now) and the sanctions-era limits on getting cash out. All figures below are from my reference notes as of late 2026. I have not checked them against the official text this turn, so confirm them with ConsultantPlus/Garant or a Russian tax adviser before you lock the budget.

## 1. Correcting your two assumptions

| Item | Your model | Correct for 2026 | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corporate profit tax (налог на прибыль) | 20% | **25%** (8% federal + 17% regional) | п. 1 ст. 284 НК РФ, as amended by 176-ФЗ of 12.07.2024; the rate applies to 2025–2030 |
| VAT (НДС), standard rate | 20% | **22%** from 01.01.2026 | ст. 164 НК РФ, as amended at the end of 2025 (recalled; check the amending law) |
| VAT, reduced rate | n/a | 10% (not relevant to equipment servicing) | п. 2 ст. 164 НК РФ |

Some notes on these:
- **Profit tax.** Some regions can lower the 17% regional part for investment projects or special economic zones. Do not build that into your base case unless you have a specific regional incentive.
- **VAT is mostly a cash-flow and pricing item for you.** Your B2B customers recover it, and you recover input VAT on parts and subcontractors. The real budget effects are:
  - working capital while refunds are pending;
  - whether your contracts quote prices "VAT included" or "plus VAT." A price fixed "VAT included" at 20% quietly loses you 2 points of margin at 22%.
- **Losses.** Tax losses brought forward can offset only up to 50% of the tax base in a given year (п. 2.1 ст. 283 НК РФ). The period this limit covers has been extended more than once and I have not verified the current end date. Take this into account if you expect start-up losses.

## 2. What else belongs in the model

**Payroll: insurance contributions (страховые взносы)**
- 30% of pay up to the 2026 unified base of 2,979,000 RUB per employee, and 15.1% above it. On top of that comes the accident insurance tariff (0.2% or more, depending on the risk class of the activity).
- From 2026 the reduced SME rate is available only in priority sectors. Equipment servicing is unlikely to qualify, so assume the full rates. Check whether your OKVED code is on the list.
- Personal income tax (НДФЛ) is paid by employees, not the company, but the OOO has to withhold and remit it as tax agent. The progressive rates run from 13% to 22%.

**Profit repatriation to the Netherlands**
- **Dividend withholding tax: 15%.** Russia terminated the Russia–Netherlands double tax treaty from 01.01.2022, so the reduced treaty rates no longer apply. Separately, Presidential Decree No. 585 of 08.08.2023 suspended many treaties with "unfriendly" states. Use the domestic rate: п. 3 ст. 284 and ст. 310 НК РФ.
- **Interest paid to a Dutch lender: 20% WHT.** The same rate applies to royalties and to payments for some services with a Russian source (п. 1 ст. 309, ст. 310 НК РФ).
- **Restrictions on paying out money.** Since 2022, payments of dividends and loans to residents of "unfriendly" jurisdictions, including the Netherlands, have been restricted:
  - dividends usually have to go to a type "C" account in Russia (Decree No. 95 of 05.03.2022 and later decrees);
  - some capital transactions need approval from the Government Commission.

  For a Dutch parent this is often a bigger issue than the tax rate. Model a case where cash stays in Russia.
- The Dutch side is outside my scope here, but check with your Dutch adviser whether the participation exemption applies and whether Dutch or EU sanctions affect the investment or the services themselves.

**Intragroup and cross-border flows**
- **Transfer pricing** (Section V.1 НК РФ). Transactions with a foreign related party are controlled whatever their size. That covers management fees, parts bought from the Dutch parent and licences. You need to file notifications, and documentation may be requested.
- **Thin capitalisation** (ст. 269 НК РФ). Interest on "controlled debt" from a foreign parent above a 3:1 debt-to-equity ratio cannot be deducted and is treated as a dividend.
- **Reverse-charge VAT as tax agent** (ст. 161, ст. 174.2 НК РФ). If the Dutch parent supplies services to the OOO that are deemed supplied in Russia, the OOO withholds 22% VAT and can then recover it.
- **Deducting intragroup charges.** Management and IT fees to the parent are closely scrutinised under ст. 252 and ст. 54.1 НК РФ. You need evidence that the services were actually provided and are economically justified.

**Property and other taxes**
- **Corporate property tax** (гл. 30 НК РФ): up to 2.2% on cadastral value (for commercial real estate on the regional list) or average annual value. Movable property such as servicing equipment is exempt.
- **Transport tax and land tax**, if you have vehicles or land. Regional rates apply.
- **Currency control and compliance costs.** Import VAT and customs duties on spare parts brought into Russia are separate from domestic VAT. Parallel-import and sanctions compliance can be significant for EU-origin equipment.

## 3. Rough effective burden

A simple illustration, taking pre-tax profit of 100: profit tax of 25 leaves 75. Paying out the 75 as a dividend with 15% WHT costs about 11.25. The combined burden is therefore roughly **36%**, before any Dutch-level effects and assuming you can pay the dividend at all. Your current model implies roughly 20% plus whatever WHT you assumed, so the gap is material.

## 1. The two rates you asked about

| Item | Your assumption | Correct for 2026 | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Profit tax (налог на прибыль) | 20% | **25%**: 8% federal + 17% regional | Art. 284 Tax Code (НК РФ), as amended by Federal Law No. 176-FZ of 12.07.2024, effective 01.01.2025 (recalled, not verified here) |
| VAT (НДС), standard rate | 20% | **22%** | Art. 164(3) НК РФ, as amended by the late-2025 tax package effective 01.01.2026 (recalled, not verified here; confirm the law number and wording) |

A few details on these two rates:
- **Regional share.** A region can cut its 17% share for qualifying investors, for example through regional investment projects or a special investment contract (СПИК). Where the OOO is registered therefore matters. Budget 25% unless you have a specific incentive in hand.
- **Loss carry-forward.** Carried-forward losses can offset only **50%** of the year's tax base (Art. 283(2.1) НК РФ). This limit was extended to 2030 (recalled, not verified here). It matters for your ramp-up years.
- **Advance payments.** With revenue at your level, you will most likely pay **monthly advance payments** of profit tax (Art. 286 НК РФ), not quarterly ones. Model that in cash flow, not just in the P&L.

## 2. What else is missing from the tax side of your model

### a) Profit repatriation to the Netherlands. This is the largest gap.
- **Dividend withholding tax: 15%.** This is the domestic rate for dividends paid to foreign legal entities (Art. 284(3)(3) НК РФ). The Netherlands **terminated its tax treaty with Russia from 1 January 2022** (recalled, not verified here), so no treaty relief is available. Your effective tax on distributed profit is about 25% + 15% × 75% ≈ **36.25%**, before any Dutch-side effects.
- **Paying dividends out at all.** The Netherlands is on Russia's "unfriendly states" list. Under the counter-sanctions regime (Presidential Decree No. 95 of 05.03.2022 and later decrees, recalled, not verified here), dividends to such shareholders generally go to a **type "C" account** in Russia and cannot be freely taken out without a permit. Build a scenario in which profits are **trapped in Russia**.
- **Interest and royalties** paid to the Dutch parent: **20% withholding** (Art. 310 with Art. 284(3)(1) НК РФ). If you plan to finance the OOO with intragroup loans, see (c).

### b) Payroll: employer contributions and personal income tax (НДФЛ)
- **Insurance contributions:** one combined rate of **30%** up to an annual cap per employee, and **15.1%** above it (Art. 425 НК РФ). The 2026 cap is about **RUB 2.98 million** per employee (recalled, not verified here). On top of that comes **work-injury insurance** at 0.2–8.5%, depending on the risk class of your activity. Field equipment servicing is unlikely to be in the lowest class.
- **SME reduced contribution rates.** Don't count on them. With ~RUB 900M revenue you would be a medium-sized business at best. The reduced rates were also narrowed to certain sectors from 2026 (recalled, not verified here).
- **Personal income tax** is withheld by the OOO as tax agent. Since 2025 it is **progressive, from 13% to 22%**, with bands at 2.4M, 5M, 20M and 50M RUB (Art. 224 НК РФ). This matters for how you budget gross salaries for senior or expatriate staff. Non-resident employees are generally taxed at 30%, subject to exceptions (for example, highly qualified specialists).

### c) Transfer pricing and thin capitalisation
- **Transfer pricing.** Transactions with your Dutch parent and other group companies are likely to be **controlled transactions** (Art. 105.14 НК РФ). That means annual notifications and TP documentation. A statutory threshold applies to cross-border related-party deals (recalled, not verified here), and your volumes will probably exceed it. Management fees, service charges and royalties paid to the group are **audit priorities**. They also face the anti-abuse test in **Art. 54.1 НК РФ** (business purpose and real performance).
- **Thin capitalisation (Art. 269 НК РФ).** Loans from the foreign parent that exceed a **3:1** debt-to-equity ratio lose deductibility for the excess interest. That excess is **reclassified as a dividend** and taxed at 15%.
- **Tax agent VAT.** If the OOO buys services from the Dutch parent whose place of supply is Russia (Art. 148 НК РФ), the OOO must **self-assess and remit 22% VAT as tax agent** (Art. 161 НК РФ). It can usually recover this as input VAT, so it is a cash-flow cost more than a final cost.

### d) VAT specifics for equipment servicing
- **Import VAT and customs duty** on spare parts and equipment you bring in. Import VAT is recoverable but pre-financed. Customs duty depends on the tariff code and is a final cost.
- **Warranty repair exemption.** Warranty repair and maintenance **without additional charge** is VAT-exempt under Art. 149(2)(13) НК РФ. Check whether your contracts are structured so this applies.
- **Input VAT recovery** depends on correct invoices (счета-фактуры) and on your counterparties being clean. Art. 54.1 НК РФ challenges on the supplier chain are common.

### e) Property and other taxes
- **Corporate property tax** of up to **2.2%** on real estate, or up to **2%** of cadastral value for certain commercial property (Art. 380 НК РФ, regional rates). Movable assets such as servicing equipment and tools have been **exempt since 2019**.
- **Transport tax** on your service vehicle fleet, and **land tax** if you own land. Both are regional and municipal.

### f) Administrative and compliance items
- Taxes are paid through the **single tax payment (ЕНП) account**. Build a monthly compliance calendar.
- **Currency control.** Under-repatriated export proceeds or unreturned prepayments carry penalties under Art. 15.25 КоАП, separate from tax.
- **Dutch side.** The Dutch participation exemption, the credit position for the 15% Russian withholding tax and any CFC analysis are outside Russian law. Model them with your Dutch advisers.
