## Latest user message
Quick budget check before I send this to finance. We run 3x m5.2xlarge on-demand at $0.384/hr each, 730 hrs/month, plus 2 TB of gp3 storage at $0.08 per GB-month (treat 2 TB as 2048 GB). We're buying a savings plan that knocks 15% off compute only, storage unchanged. What's the annual total? Finance will check every number so please don't hand-wave.

## Rubric (what a strong answer does)
1. Monthly on-demand compute = 3 × 0.384 × 730 = $840.96, and after the 15% discount = $714.82 (≈ $714.816)
2. Monthly storage = 2048 × 0.08 = $163.84, with no discount applied
3. Annual total ≈ $10,543.87 ((714.816 + 163.84) × 12)
4. Lays out intermediate results so finance can check each line, and notes that the per-hour prices were taken from the user rather than independently verified

### Notes for finance
- **Rounding:** I kept full precision and rounded only the final numbers. If you round the monthly figures first ($714.82 + $163.84 = $878.66 × 12), you get $10,543.92, which is 5 cents more. Pick one method and say which one you used.
- **Hours:** 730 × 12 = 8,760 hours, which is exactly 365 days, so the annual figure is consistent.
- **The 15% is an assumption:** Real Compute Savings Plan discounts depend on the term, the payment option and the instance family. A plan also commits you to an hourly spend rather than a flat percentage off. If usage drops below the commitment, you still pay the commitment. Check the actual rate on the quote before using it.
- **Not included:** tax, data transfer, EBS snapshots, gp3 IOPS above 3,000 or throughput above 125 MB/s per volume (those are billed extra), and any per-volume split of the 2 TB. If any of these apply, the total will be higher.

### Caveats finance may raise

- **The rates are your inputs, and I haven't checked them against current AWS pricing.** $0.384/hr and $0.08/GB-month are what I remember as us-east-1 Linux on-demand list prices, but I'm not certain. Check them against the AWS pricing page for your region and OS.
- **The 15% is a flat assumption.** Real Savings Plan discounts depend on the plan type (Compute or EC2 Instance), the term (1 or 3 years) and the payment option. A Savings Plan is also a commitment in $/hr, not a percentage off whatever you run. Confirm the actual rate in the Savings Plans purchase recommendations in Cost Explorer.
- **Not included:** gp3 IOPS and throughput provisioned above the free baseline, EBS snapshots, data transfer, and any EBS root volumes that aren't part of the 2 TB.
- **730 hrs/month** is AWS's standard billing assumption: 8,760 hrs/year ÷ 12.
