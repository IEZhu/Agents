**On-call checklist: API release**

- [ ] **1. Check the deployment status:**
  `kubectl rollout status deployment/api -n prod`
- [ ] **2. Roll back if 5xx errors have more than doubled within 10 minutes of the release:**
  `kubectl rollout undo deployment/api -n prod`
- [ ] **3. Post in #incidents** what was rolled back and the time it happened.
- [ ] **4. Do not restart the database** unless the on-call DBA has approved it.
- [ ] **5. Once things are stable, open a ticket** for the incident review.
