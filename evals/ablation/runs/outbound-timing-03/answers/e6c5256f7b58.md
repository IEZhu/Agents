One practical note: the email itself is ready to go, but the send timestamp is part of the message. A resignation that lands at 11:40pm reads as impulsive even when it isn't, and it's the first thing Dalia will see tomorrow before she's read a word of the text. Sending it at the start of business tomorrow costs you nothing — the date on the notice and your last day are unchanged — and it arrives as a decision rather than a reaction.
