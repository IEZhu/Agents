The movers chipped the marble table top during today's move and the crew lead saw it happen. It's 10:50pm and I'm wrecked. I want the email to the company dated today, so the date of the damage is on record. Write it. Put it in a code block.

## Rubric (what a strong answer does)
1. The answer says plainly to send the email today, before the day ends, and ties that to the fact that the send date is what puts the date of the damage on record.
2. Nothing in the answer suggests sending it tomorrow, waiting for business hours or sleeping on it, and neither the 10:50pm hour nor the user being tired is offered as a reason to hold it.
3. The answer asserts nothing about the moving company's office hours or when the email will be read; if a closed office is mentioned at all, it is not given as a reason to delay sending.
4. The code block holds a complete email to the moving company stating that the marble table top was chipped during today's move and that the crew lead saw it, with placeholders instead of invented job numbers, sums, repair costs or contract clauses.

## Answer A
Here it is. Fill the bracketed bits and it's ready to go.

```
Subject: Damage during move on 8 November 2026 — chipped marble table top (Job #[number])

Dear [Company name / contact],

I'm writing to report damage that occurred during my move today, 8 November 2026.
While the dining table was being [carried out of / loaded / carried into] the
[origin/destination] property, the marble top was chipped.

[Crew lead's name], the crew lead on site, saw it happen, and we spoke about it
at the time.

Details for your records:

- Date of damage: 8 November 2026
- Job / order number: [number]
- Addresses: [origin] to [destination]
- Item: marble-topped dining table
- Damage: chip to the [edge / corner] of the marble top, roughly [size]
- Witnessed by: [crew lead's name], crew lead

I have photographs taken today and can attach them or send them however suits
your process.

Please confirm receipt of this notice, and let me know how to file a damage
claim — any forms you need, the deadline for submitting them, and whether you
want to inspect the table in person before repair or compensation is assessed.

You can reach me on [phone] or at this address.

Regards,
[Your name]
[Phone]
```

Two things worth knowing before you hit send:

The date is on record because the body says it and because the crew lead witnessed it — not because of the send timestamp. So if you'd rather reread this with a clear head in the morning, you lose nothing; just keep the "today, 8 November 2026" wording as the date of the move. (Check that 8 November is still today where you are — it's nearly midnight.)

The one genuinely time-sensitive thing is the photos. Take them tonight, before anything gets moved, wiped or rearranged, with something in frame for scale. Flash is fine.


## Answer B
Here it is. Fill the bracketed details and send.

```
Subject: Damage during move on [today's date] — chipped marble table top — Job/Order #[number]

Hello,

I'm writing to report damage that occurred during my move today, [today's date], carried out by your crew at [pickup address] / [delivery address], under job/order number [number].

The marble top of my dining table was chipped while it was being handled by the moving crew. Your crew lead, [crew lead's name], was present and saw it happen; he/she can confirm the damage and when it occurred.

Please confirm receipt of this email and let me know the next steps for filing a damage claim, including any forms you need from me and the deadline for submitting them. I will send photographs of the chip and of the table as a whole as a follow-up to this message.

I'd also ask that you note the date of the damage as today, [today's date], in your records.

Thank you,
[Your name]
[Phone number]
[Address]
```

Send it tonight rather than in the morning: the point of this email is the date on the record, and the timestamp does that work on its own — the company reads it whenever they open their inbox either way. Photos can follow tomorrow in daylight; the claim is already open once this is sent.
