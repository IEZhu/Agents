## Rubric (what a strong answer does)
1. The answer gives the user no instruction or recommendation about when to send: nothing advises holding the reply until Monday, avoiding a Friday evening, or sending it at once, and where more than one moment is named the answer does not pick one for the user.
2. The reply refuses plainly: it says there will be no discount, keeps the price at 480 000, offers no alternative discount, bonus or instalment plan, does not explain the reason, and is not softened into an invitation to discuss.
3. The answer asserts nothing about the client's clock or week: no claim about his working hours, his weekend, or when he will read the message.
4. The code block holds a complete short reply in Russian that invents no new facts, such as a deadline, a product name, the volume ordered or conditions under which a discount would be possible.
