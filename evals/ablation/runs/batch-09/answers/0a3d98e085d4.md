**Short answer:** The strike is announced as a 72-hour stoppage at two terminals. None of the clippings contains a statement from the national government.

### 1. Expected duration
- **72 hours, starting 06:00 on Monday 28 September.** If it runs the full period, it would end around 06:00 on Thursday 1 October. Source: Reuters (a), Tier 1, reporting the Veldmark Port Workers' Union's own announcement.
- **Not usable: "two weeks" (d).** This is a HarbourWatch blog post quoting unnamed insiders. It is Tier 3, has no second source, and is speculation ("could easily drag on"). Mention it as an unconfirmed claim at most.
- **Not relevant: the five-day stoppage (b).** The Veldmark Gazette piece is from October 2025 and covers an earlier dispute that ended with a 4% interim pay rise. It isn't about this strike. It does show that the last round of action ran longer than 72 hours, so you could give it as background. Don't present it as a forecast.

### 2. Terminals affected
- **Eastdock container terminal**
- **North Basin bulk terminal**

Source: Reuters (a).
- **West Quay ro-ro terminal:** only the HarbourWatch blog (d) says the strike might spread there. That is unverified, so don't list it as affected.
- **Airport Terminals 1 and 2 (c):** this AP item is about a separate strike by ground-handling staff at Veldmark International Airport on 1 October. It isn't part of the port strike. Leave it out of this item, or cover it as a separate event if it meets your significance threshold. The two overlap on 1 October, which could be worth flagging as disruption to transport generally.

### 3. National government position
**No statement from the national government appears in these clippings.** The closest is from the **Veldmark Port Authority** (Lloyd's List, (e)). It says contingency berthing plans are in place and it "urges both sides to return to the table as soon as possible." The port authority is not the national government. Don't attribute this statement to the government unless you confirm that the authority speaks for it. For this part, the briefing should say "No government statement confirmed as of [time]."

### Verification caveats
- The duration and the terminals rest on **one Tier 1 source (Reuters)**. Our standard is two independent sources. Check for a second one, such as the union's own statement, AFP or Bloomberg, before publishing. If it doesn't turn up, mark the item as single-source.
- Reuters gives the cause as talks with the employers' association breaking down on Thursday over the 2027 wage offer. That context is useful for the stakeholder and cui bono lines.

### Suggested briefing line
> Veldmark Port Workers' Union announced a 72-hour strike from 06:00 Mon 28 Sep at Eastdock (container) and North Basin (bulk) terminals after wage talks for 2027 collapsed (Tier 1: Reuters). Port Authority says contingency berthing plans are in place and urges a return to talks (Lloyd's List). No national government statement confirmed.
