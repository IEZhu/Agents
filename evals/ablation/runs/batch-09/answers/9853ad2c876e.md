### Notes for the argument
- **Only the other three count.** When you look for the player with the fewest coins, you don't include yourself. For example, in Round 3 Ben has 4 after giving, but that doesn't matter until someone else's turn.
- **The alphabetical tie-break never came up.** On every turn exactly one other player had the fewest coins, so that rule didn't affect the result.
- **The pattern repeats.** The coins move around the circle the same way every round: Anna to Dan, Ben to Anna, Cleo to Ben, Dan to Cleo. After each round Dan ends with 5 and the players tied for the most pay the bank, so the total in play keeps shrinking.
