## Latest user message
Quick sanity check before I send numbers to finance. We surveyed all 120 engineers about which of our three trackers they actively use: Jira, Linear, Notion. Results as they came back from the survey tool:

- 70 use Jira
- 45 use Linear
- 50 use Notion
- 20 use both Jira and Linear
- 17 use Jira and Notion but not Linear
- 15 use both Linear and Notion
- 8 use all three

I need: (1) how many people use exactly one of the three tools, (2) how many use none of them, and (3) if we kill Linear and move everyone on it to Jira, how many new Jira seats do we need to buy? The "both" counts include people who use all three.

## Rubric (what a strong answer does)
1. Converts the 17 'Jira and Notion but not Linear' into a full Jira-Notion overlap of 25 (17 + 8) rather than using 17 as |J∩N|
2. Reports 69 people using exactly one tool (33 only Jira, 18 only Linear, 18 only Notion) and 7 using none (union of 113)
3. Reports 25 new Jira seats (45 Linear users minus the 20 who already use Jira)
4. Shows the region breakdown or set formulas so finance can check the numbers, and the regions sum to 120

## Answer A
**Answers:** (1) **69** use exactly one tool, (2) **7** use none, (3) **25** new Jira seats.

**One thing to watch:** the Jira + Notion line is worded differently from the others. It says "17 use Jira and Notion **but not Linear**," so it leaves out the 8 people who use all three. The other two pair counts include them, as you said. So the full Jira ∩ Notion count is 17 + 8 = **25**, not 17.

### The breakdown

| Region | Calculation | Count |
|---|---|---|
| All three | given | 8 |
| Jira + Linear only | 20 − 8 | 12 |
| Jira + Notion only | given (already excludes Linear) | 17 |
| Linear + Notion only | 15 − 8 | 7 |
| Jira only | 70 − 12 − 17 − 8 | 33 |
| Linear only | 45 − 12 − 7 − 8 | 18 |
| Notion only | 50 − 17 − 7 − 8 | 18 |
| **Use at least one** | sum of the above | **113** |
| **None** | 120 − 113 | **7** |

Check with inclusion–exclusion: 70 + 45 + 50 − 20 − 25 − 15 + 8 = 113. The two methods agree.

1. **Exactly one tool:** 33 + 18 + 18 = **69**
2. **None:** **7**
3. **New Jira seats:** everyone on Linear who isn't already on Jira, which is 45 − 20 = **25**. The 20 Jira + Linear users already have Jira seats.

### Why the reading matters

If you take 17 as the full Jira ∩ Notion count, including triple users, you get 70 + 45 + 50 − 20 − 17 − 15 + 8 = **121** users. That's more than your 120 engineers, so that reading can't be right, and anyone in finance who checks the math will catch it. The same misreading would also give "exactly one" as 85 instead of 69. Answer (3) is 25 under either reading.

### Before sending

- **Seats vs. users:** the 25 assumes you currently have exactly 70 paid Jira seats, one per active user. If you already have spare licenses, subtract them. If Jira bills in tiers, round up to the next tier.
- **Survey data:** "actively use" is self-reported. Linear's admin panel will have the actual list of active Linear users, which is a better number to base a purchase on than survey responses.


## Answer B
**Answers: (1) 69 use exactly one tool, (2) 7 use none, (3) 25 new Jira seats.**

One thing to check before this goes to finance: the Jira + Notion line is worded differently from the other two overlap lines. "20 Jira and Linear" and "15 Linear and Notion" include the 8 who use all three. "17 Jira and Notion **but not Linear**" does not. So the full Jira ∩ Notion count is 17 + 8 = **25**, not 17.

That reading is the only one that works. If you treat 17 as the full Jira ∩ Notion count, the union comes to 70 + 45 + 50 − 20 − 17 − 15 + 8 = 121 engineers, which is more than the 120 you surveyed.

### Breakdown (Venn regions)

| Region | Calculation | Count |
|---|---|---|
| All three | given | 8 |
| Jira + Linear only | 20 − 8 | 12 |
| Linear + Notion only | 15 − 8 | 7 |
| Jira + Notion only | given ("but not Linear") | 17 |
| Jira only | 70 − 12 − 17 − 8 | 33 |
| Linear only | 45 − 12 − 7 − 8 | 18 |
| Notion only | 50 − 17 − 7 − 8 | 18 |
| **Use at least one** | sum of the regions above | **113** |
| **None** | 120 − 113 | **7** |

Cross-check with inclusion–exclusion: 70 + 45 + 50 − 20 − 25 − 15 + 8 = 113. ✔

### The three answers

1. **Exactly one tool:** 33 + 18 + 18 = **69**
2. **None:** **7**
3. **New Jira seats if Linear is killed:** Linear users who aren't already on Jira = 45 − 20 = **25** (18 Linear-only + 7 Linear + Notion). The 20 who already use Jira need no new seat.

### Caveats for finance

- **25 is a headcount, not a purchase order.** It assumes every Jira seat you pay for today is used. If your license has unused seats, you need to buy fewer. Compare 25 against (licensed seats − 70).
- **"Actively use" is self-reported.** Survey answers and license or login data often disagree. If you can pull last-login data from Linear, check the 25 against it before committing.
- Whether the 7 non-users will need Jira later isn't something this data can answer.
