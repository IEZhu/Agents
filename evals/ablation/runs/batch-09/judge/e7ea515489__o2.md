## Latest user message
My kids made up a coin game and now they're arguing about the result, so I need a neutral referee. Four players: Anna 12 coins, Ben 7, Cleo 9, Dan 4.

Rules for one round: players act in order Anna, Ben, Cleo, Dan. On your turn you give half of your current coins (rounded down) to whichever other player has the fewest coins at that exact moment; if there's a tie for fewest, give to the one who comes first alphabetically. After all four have acted, whoever has the most coins pays 1 coin to the bank; if several are tied for most, each of them pays 1.

They played 3 rounds. How many coins does each kid have at the end, and how many are in the bank?

## Rubric (what a strong answer does)
1. Shows the state of all four players after each individual turn (or at least after each round) so the trace can be checked
2. Correctly applies the tie rule at the bank step: after round 2 Anna and Cleo both pay (tied at 9), after round 3 Anna, Ben and Cleo all pay (tied at 8)
3. Final answer: Anna 7, Ben 7, Cleo 7, Dan 5, bank 6
4. Final totals are consistent (26 coins with players plus 6 in the bank equals the starting 32)

## Answer A
**Final result after 3 rounds: Anna 7, Ben 7, Cleo 7, Dan 5, Bank 6.**

The counts check out: 7 + 7 + 7 + 5 + 6 = 32, the same as the 12 + 7 + 9 + 4 = 32 coins they started with.

Below is the full trace, so the kids can check each move. "Fewest" always means the fewest among the *other* players, because you can't give coins to yourself.

### Round 1 (start: A 12, B 7, C 9, D 4)

| Turn | Gives | To (fewest at that moment) | After: A / B / C / D |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anna (12) | 6 | Dan (4) | 6 / 7 / 9 / 10 |
| Ben (7) | 3 | Anna (6) | 9 / 4 / 9 / 10 |
| Cleo (9) | 4 | Ben (4) | 9 / 8 / 5 / 10 |
| Dan (10) | 5 | Cleo (5) | 9 / 8 / 10 / 5 |

Most coins: Cleo with 10, so she pays 1. The totals are now **A 9, B 8, C 9, D 5, bank 1**.

### Round 2

|---|---|---|---|

| Ben (8) | 4 | Anna (5) | 9 / 4 / 9 / 9 |
| Cleo (9) | 4 | Ben (4) | 9 / 8 / 5 / 9 |
| Dan (9) | 4 | Cleo (5) | 9 / 8 / 9 / 5 |

Most coins: Anna and Cleo are tied with 9, so each pays 1. The totals are now **A 8, B 8, C 8, D 5, bank 3**.

### Round 3

| Turn | Gives | To | After: A / B / C / D |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anna (8) | 4 | Dan (5) | 4 / 8 / 8 / 9 |

Most coins: Anna, Ben and Cleo are tied with 8, so each pays 1. The totals are now **A 7, B 7, C 7, D 5, bank 6**.

### Notes for the referee
- None of the "fewest" choices in this game ended in a tie, so the alphabetical tie-break never came into play. The only ties were for *most* coins, at the end of rounds 2 and 3.
- The rounding down only mattered twice: Ben's 7 gave 3, and Dan's 9 gave 4. Those are the most likely places for someone to have counted differently.
- A pattern shows up: each player passes coins to the player who just gave theirs away, so the coins go around in a circle. Dan always gets half of Anna's coins and hands them straight back out, so he ends every round with exactly 5.


## Answer B
**Final result after 3 rounds: Anna 7, Ben 7, Cleo 7, Dan 5, and the bank has 6.**

Check: 7 + 7 + 7 + 5 + 6 = 32, which is the 12 + 7 + 9 + 4 = 32 coins they started with.

Here is every move so the kids can check it themselves. In each row, "gives" means half of the giver's current coins, rounded down. It goes to whoever has the fewest coins among the *other* three players at that moment.

### Round 1 (start: A 12, B 7, C 9, D 4)

| Turn | Gives | To (fewest) | Anna | Ben | Cleo | Dan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Anna (12) | 6 | Dan (4) | 6 | 7 | 9 | 10 |
| Ben (7) | 3 | Anna (6) | 9 | 4 | 9 | 10 |
| Cleo (9) | 4 | Ben (4) | 9 | 8 | 5 | 10 |
| Dan (10) | 5 | Cleo (5) | 9 | 8 | 10 | 5 |
| Bank | Cleo has the most (10) and pays 1 | | **9** | **8** | **9** | **5** |

Bank: 1

### Round 2

| Turn | Gives | To (fewest) | Anna | Ben | Cleo | Dan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Anna (9) | 4 | Dan (5) | 5 | 8 | 9 | 9 |
| Ben (8) | 4 | Anna (5) | 9 | 4 | 9 | 9 |
| Cleo (9) | 4 | Ben (4) | 9 | 8 | 5 | 9 |
| Dan (9) | 4 | Cleo (5) | 9 | 8 | 9 | 5 |
| Bank | Anna and Cleo tie for most (9) and each pays 1 | | **8** | **8** | **8** | **5** |

Bank: 3

### Round 3

| Turn | Gives | To (fewest) | Anna | Ben | Cleo | Dan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Anna (8) | 4 | Dan (5) | 4 | 8 | 8 | 9 |
| Ben (8) | 4 | Anna (4) | 8 | 4 | 8 | 9 |
| Cleo (8) | 4 | Ben (4) | 8 | 8 | 4 | 9 |
| Dan (9) | 4 | Cleo (4) | 8 | 8 | 8 | 5 |
| Bank | Anna, Ben and Cleo tie for most (8) and each pays 1 | | **7** | **7** | **7** | **5** |

Bank: 6

### Notes for the argument
- **Only the other three count.** When you look for the player with the fewest coins, you don't include yourself. For example, in Round 3 Ben has 4 after giving, but that doesn't matter until someone else's turn.
- **The alphabetical tie-break never came up.** On every turn exactly one other player had the fewest coins, so that rule didn't affect the result.
- **The pattern repeats.** The coins move around the circle the same way every round: Anna to Dan, Ben to Anna, Cleo to Ben, Dan to Cleo. After each round Dan ends with 5 and the players tied for the most pay the bank, so the total in play keeps shrinking.
