## Points to watch

- **Income tax on the rent depends on your other income.** Dividends are exempt from income tax, so the rent is currently your only taxable income, and it falls under the threshold. If you also pay yourself a director's salary, the rent is added on top of it and taxed at your marginal band. You can still deduct interest on any loan used to buy the flats.
- **Timing of the dividend.** You can't declare a final dividend out of 2026 profits until they are established. Doing it "this year" means an **interim dividend** backed by interim accounts, as the articles and Cap. 113 allow. Otherwise the payout slips into 2027. Any dividend paid in 2026 out of **2025 or earlier profits** gets the 5% SDC rate, but those profits were taxed at 12.5% CIT. That can be worth using if there are retained earnings.
- **Old retained earnings.** If the company has undistributed profits from 2022–2025, check how the transitional deemed-distribution rules treat them. A 2026 dividend may be the cheapest way to clear them.
- **Interest SDC was not cut.** It stays at 17% for domiciled residents, so it matters if you hold large personal deposits.
- **Rates are recalled, not verified.** I'm confident about the direction of every change above, but check the exact percentages, the GHS cap and the new income tax bands against the enacted amending laws before relying on them.
