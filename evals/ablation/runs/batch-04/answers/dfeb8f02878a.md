Here's the 4-week chest/bench block. One thing first, because I think it's the main reason you're stuck: **the plan isn't short on effort, it's short on recovery.**

## Why 100x5 isn't moving

- **Every set to failure, twice a week.** That's about 16 chest sets per session and ~32 per week, all at failure. Recent meta-analyses I recall (e.g., Grgic et al. 2022, Refalo et al. 2023; recalled, not verified here) found that going to failure doesn't clearly beat stopping 1–3 reps short for hypertrophy. It does add a lot of fatigue. For strength it tends to hurt: bar speed and technique break down, and your next session starts in a hole.
- **~6h sleep.** You can't train through that the way you could on 8h. Sleep restriction is fairly consistently linked to worse recovery and strength performance, so volume has to come down to match.
- **Achy shoulders.** Dips plus flyes plus bench plus incline, all to failure, is a classic recipe for anterior shoulder irritation. "Nothing major" right now is exactly when to fix it.

"It doesn't feel like I worked" is a feeling, not a stimulus. For 4 weeks, judge a session by how fast the bar moves, not by how wrecked you feel.

## Rules for this block

- **Compounds at RPE 7–8** (2–3 reps left in the tank). No grinding.
- **Failure only on the last set of flyes.** Nowhere else.
- **Dips are out** until the shoulders are quiet. Close-grip bench replaces them: it trains triceps and transfers directly to bench.
- **Autoregulate:** if the top set feels RPE 9+, drop 2.5 kg for the back-off sets. If it feels RPE 6 or lower, add 2.5 kg next week.
- Weekly chest volume drops from ~32 sets to about 17–18. That's intentional.

Loads assume your 100x5 was near failure, which puts your estimated 1RM at roughly 112–115 kg.

## Push A: Heavy bench day

**1. Barbell Bench Press: top set + back-offs**
Tempo 2-1-X-0 (controlled descent, brief touch, drive hard)

| Week | Top set | Back-off sets | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1x3 @ 97.5 kg (RPE 7–8) | 3x5 @ 90 kg (RPE 7) | 3 min |
| 2 | 1x3 @ 100 kg (RPE 8) | 3x5 @ 92.5 kg (RPE 7–8) | 3 min |
| 3 | 1x3 @ 102.5 kg (RPE 8) | 3x5 @ 95 kg (RPE 8) | 3 min |
| 4 | **Test:** 1x5 @ 100 kg (target RPE 8–9) | 2x5 @ 90 kg | 3–4 min |

**2. Incline DB Press (~30°):** 3x8–10, RPE 8, rest 2 min. Add weight when all 3 sets hit 10 at RPE ≤8.

**3. Cable Fly (high-to-low or mid):** 2x12–15, rest 60–75 s. Set 1 at RPE 8, **set 2 to failure** (this is where you get your "worked" feeling).

## Push B: Technique + triceps day

**1. Paused Bench Press** (2-second pause on the chest, no sinking or bouncing)

| Week | Protocol | Rest |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3x4 @ 85 kg (RPE 7) | 2.5 min |
| 2 | 3x4 @ 87.5 kg (RPE 7–8) | 2.5 min |
| 3 | 3x4 @ 90 kg (RPE 8) | 2.5 min |
| 4 | 2x4 @ 80 kg (easy, deload) | 2 min |

The pause removes the stretch reflex and builds strength off the chest. Most 5-rep bench stalls happen there.

**2. Close-Grip Bench Press** (hands about shoulder-width, elbows tucked ~30–45°)
- W1 3x6 @ 75 kg · W2 3x6 @ 77.5 kg · W3 3x6 @ 80 kg · W4 2x6 @ 70 kg
- RPE 7–8, rest 2 min

**3. Cable Fly or Pec Deck:** 2x12–15, last set to failure (W4: 1 set at RPE 7).

**Week 4 order:** do Push B (the light session) first, then the test on Push A. You go into the test fresh.

## Technique checks for every bench set

1. Shoulder blades pulled back and down, held there the whole set. This is your best shoulder-protection tool.
2. Elbows at roughly 45–70° from the torso, not flared to 90°.
3. Bar touches around the lower sternum and travels slightly back toward the face on the way up.
4. Feet planted, leg drive into the floor, glutes on the bench.
5. Film a set from the side each week. If bar speed on rep 5 looks like a grind, you were at RPE 9+.

## Two things outside chest

- **Pull days:** keep plenty of rows and add 3x15–20 face pulls or band pull-aparts on both pull days. A strong upper back gives you a stable bench platform and helps the shoulders.
- **Sleep:** if you can get to 7h even a few nights a week, it will likely do more for your bench than any set/rep change here.

## After week 4

- **100x5 moved well (RPE ≤8):** run the block again starting at 100 kg for the top set.
- **Hit it but it was a grind:** repeat weeks 2–3 before retesting.
- **Missed it:** that's probably still fatigue from the old plan. Run weeks 1–3 again. Don't go back to failure training.

## Shoulder red flags: stop pressing and see a physio or sports doctor if you get

- Sharp pain (not just achiness) during the lowering phase of bench or at the bottom
- Pain at night or when lying on that shoulder
- Clicking with pain, or noticeable loss of strength raising the arm
- Aching that gets worse week to week despite the lower volume

Not medical advice. Consult a doctor if pain occurs.
