## Part 1 — Clean up before the move (do this now, September–October)

### A. The freelance designer (highest priority)

1. **Retención en la fuente on her payments**
   - Payments to individuals for honorarios or services are generally subject to withholding. The SAS is the withholding agent (agente de retención).
   - As I recall, Art. 383 E.T. (parágrafo 2) says that for an individual earning honorarios who hasn't hired 2 or more workers, the **progressive employment-income table** applies instead of a flat rate. Under that table you first subtract her mandatory contributions and the 25% renta exenta. The other route is the flat honorarios rate of roughly 10–11% (recalled, not verified).
   - At COP 5M/month, the table method could give a low or even **zero** withholding. **You still have to run the calculation for every payment and keep the working papers.** Ask her in writing whether she has hired 2 or more workers, because the answer decides which method applies.
   - If withholding was due and you didn't apply it, **the SAS answers for it** (Art. 370 E.T.). Fixing it means amending the Form 350 withholding returns for the affected months and paying the tax plus interest and a correction penalty. Your contador should do this calculation first.

2. **Documento soporte electrónico**
   - A cuenta de cobro is not enough to deduct a purchase from someone who isn't required to invoice. DIAN requires the buyer to issue an **electronic support document** (documento soporte en adquisiciones a sujetos no obligados a facturar), under DIAN Resolución 000167 de 2021 and Art. 771-2 E.T. (recalled, verify current version).
   - Without it, DIAN can reject the deduction for **COP 35M+** of expenses since March.
   - Issue the documents for the past months now. Check with your contador or software provider about any penalty for issuing them late.
   - Also look at her RUT. If she is registered as **responsable de IVA**, she must issue *factura electrónica* herself, and a cuenta de cobro is wrong for that reason too.

3. **Her social security contributions (salud, pensión, ARL)**
   - As I understand the current rule, Art. 108 E.T. makes payments to independent contractors deductible only if the contracting party **checks that the contractor has paid her contributions**. Those contributions are calculated on 40% of the monthly contract value, per Art. 244 Ley 1955 de 2019 (recalled).
   - **In practice:** ask for her paid PILA planilla for the month before you release each payment, and keep it on file. Ask for the planillas going back to March now.
   - For ARL, Decreto 723 de 2013 (recalled) requires contractors on contracts longer than one month to be affiliated. The contracting company usually handles the affiliation. Confirm this with the ARL you use for your employees.

4. **Contrato realidad (misclassification) risk**
   - She has worked continuously for seven months for a fixed monthly amount. If she also works set hours, takes day-to-day orders or works like staff, a court or the **UGPP** could treat her as an employee (Arts. 23–24 CST, where the presumption of an employment contract applies). You would then owe back prestaciones sociales, vacations and contributions, plus penalties.
   - **Fix:** put a written *contrato de prestación de servicios* in place with defined deliverables, her own schedule and tools, and no subordination. Make sure the day-to-day reality matches the contract. If she really works as staff, it may be cheaper to hire her.

5. **Exógena:** payments to her have to be reported in your annual información exógena filing. Make sure her NIT/cédula details are correct.

### B. Filings that may have been missed since June

With no accountant since June, check each of these for July, August and September:
- **Form 350** (monthly withholding return), for payroll withholding and any other withholding.
- **IVA returns** (Form 300), bimonthly or four-monthly depending on your income.
- **Nómina electrónica**, the monthly electronic payroll support transmitted to DIAN.
- **PILA** for the 3 employees. It's probably fine if the payroll operator runs it, but confirm.
- **ICA Medellín**, including the filing frequency and any ICA withholding (retención de ICA) you're required to apply.
- **Accounting books kept up to date.** Your financial statements must be certified by the legal representative **and a contador público** (Art. 37 Ley 222 de 1995), so you can't run this for long without one.

### C. IVA on your SaaS

Confirm how IVA applies to your subscriptions. Certain **cloud computing services are excluded from IVA** under Art. 476 E.T. (recalled), but only if specific conditions are met. Sales to foreign customers may also get different treatment. If you've been charging 19% when the service qualifies for the exclusion, or not charging it when it doesn't, fix that before you change jurisdiction.

## Part 2 — Relocation checklist

### Corporate / registry
- [ ] **Check your bylaws (estatutos).** If the domicilio social is Medellín, changing it is a **bylaws amendment** (reforma estatutaria) and needs a shareholders' meeting decision with the quorum and majority the bylaws require. Record it in the minutes (acta).
- [ ] Register the amendment with the **Cámara de Comercio de Medellín para Antioquia**. The file is then transferred to the **Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá**. Budget for registration fees and the departmental registration tax (impuesto de registro).
- [ ] Update the registered **commercial and notification addresses** (dirección comercial and dirección de notificación judicial).
- [ ] Remember that the **2027 matrícula mercantil renewal** is due by **31 March** (Art. 33 C.Co.), and it will be in Bogotá.
- [ ] Alternative to consider: if the change of domicile is complicated, you could keep the Medellín domicile and just open operations in Bogotá. For a 3-person company, a clean move is usually simpler.

### DIAN / national tax
- [ ] **Update the RUT** with the new address and the corresponding dirección seccional. Do it promptly, because late updates carry a per-day penalty under Art. 658-3 E.T. (recalled).
- [ ] Update the address on your **electronic invoices**, nómina electrónica and documento soporte templates with your provider.
- [ ] Make sure the change is reflected in your 2026 income tax return, filed in 2027.

### Municipal tax (ICA)
- [ ] **Medellín:** report the cese de actividades / cancellation with the Secretaría de Hacienda de Medellín, then file the final ICA return and pay what you owe.
- [ ] **Bogotá:** register in the **RIT** (Registro de Información Tributaria) with the Secretaría Distrital de Hacienda **before** you start operating there. Check the ICA rate and filing frequency for your CIIU code. Rates vary by city, so don't reuse the Medellín numbers.
- [ ] Split your ICA income correctly between Medellín and Bogotá for the transition period.

### Employees
- [ ] **Review each employment contract.** If the place of work is fixed as Medellín, moving it is a material change that needs the employee's **written consent**. You may also have to pay relocation costs; look at Art. 57 CST.
- [ ] For anyone who won't move: agree to **remote work** (trabajo remoto, Ley 2121 de 2021, or teletrabajo, Ley 1221 de 2008) with a written addendum. The other route is a negotiated exit. Ending a contract without just cause triggers the indemnización under Art. 64 CST.
- [ ] **Caja de compensación:** these are regional, so you'll probably need to move from the Antioquia caja to a Bogotá one for employees working there. Confirm the rules for remote staff who stay in Antioquia.
- [ ] **ARL:** register the new work site and update the risk classification.
- [ ] **SG-SST:** update your occupational health and safety system for the new premises.
- [ ] EPS and pension funds are national, so they shouldn't change. Just update the employer address.

### Office lease
- [ ] **El Poblado lease:** read the termination clause, the notice period and any early-exit penalty now. Commercial leases fall under Arts. 518–524 C.Co., and the contract terms control how you as tenant can leave. Give notice in writing, arrange the move-out inspection and set a date for getting the deposit back.
- [ ] **Bogotá premises:** before you sign, check that **zoning (uso de suelo)** allows your activity at that address.

### Commercial / data / operations
- [ ] Update the legal address in your **SaaS terms of service, privacy policy and DPA**. Under **Ley 1581 de 2012**, your privacy policy has to identify the data controller and its contact details correctly.
- [ ] Notify your bank, payment processors, customers (the address appears on invoices) and suppliers, and update your website.
- [ ] Update notification addresses in customer contracts.

### Professional support
- [ ] **Hire a contador público before January.** Beyond the certification rule, amending the withholding returns and closing out ICA in Medellín while registering in Bogotá is exactly the work a contador should do.
- [ ] Check whether you've crossed the thresholds that require a **revisor fiscal** (Ley 43 de 1990, Art. 13 par. 2, based on assets and income expressed in SMMLV). A small SaaS company probably hasn't, but confirm.
