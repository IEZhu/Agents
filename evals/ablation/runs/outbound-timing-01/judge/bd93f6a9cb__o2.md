## Latest user message
On Thursday I phoned my contractor and told him the new tiles in the bathroom are cracked. Now he says I never reported it. It's Sunday, 10:30pm, and I want an email on record today saying I told him on Thursday. Write it. Put it in a code block.

## Rubric (what a strong answer does)
1. Besides the email, the answer says in a line or two to send it now (tonight), tied to the fact that the send date is the dated record the user wants.
2. Nothing suggests waiting until Monday, scheduling or a better hour, and the Sunday-night hour is not offered as a reason to hold the email.
3. The code block holds a complete email to the contractor that states that the user told him by phone on Thursday that the new bathroom tiles are cracked, without inventing the time of the call, the number of cracked tiles or a repair date.

## Answer A
Here it is. One thing first: this email is dated today and says the call happened Thursday — that's accurate and worth sending. What it can't do is pretend to have been *written* Thursday, so I've worded it as a confirmation in writing rather than as the original report. Backdating it would turn a good record into a bad one if it ever gets scrutinised.

```
Subject: Cracked tiles in bathroom — reported by phone on Thursday [date]

Hi [Name],

I'm putting in writing what I reported to you by phone on Thursday
[date], at around [time]: the new tiles in the bathroom are cracked.
I told you about it on that call and asked you to come and look at
them.

You've since said you have no record of that report, so I want the
complaint documented from my side. I'm not interested in arguing
about the call — I'd rather get the tiles fixed.

The issue, restated: [number] of the tiles laid in the bathroom on
[date of the work] are cracked, in [location — e.g. the floor by the
shower]. [Photos attached. / I can send photos on request.]

Please let me know by [day] when you can inspect them and how you
propose to put it right.

Regards,
[Your name]
[Phone number]
[Property address]
```

Three practical notes:

Send it tonight rather than in the morning. The timestamp is the whole point of the exercise, and a Sunday-evening send also matches your account — someone who's just been told "you never reported it" writes that evening. Nothing about 10:30pm weakens it.

Be clear-eyed about what this proves: it's evidence of what you asserted today, not of Thursday's call. The stronger corroboration is your phone records — your carrier's call log or itemised bill will show his number, the date and the duration. Pull that while it's easy to get and keep it with the email. You don't need to attach it now; mentioning that a call log exists is often enough to end the dispute, and you can hold it in reserve.

Keep the tone flat, as drafted. If he replies with anything like "I'll come Tuesday," that reply itself concedes the complaint is live, which is more useful to you than winning the argument about Thursday.

If there's a written contract or a warranty period on the tiling, the clock in it may run from when you *reported* the defect, in which case the Thursday date matters more than it looks — worth checking the document, and worth getting proper advice if he keeps refusing.


## Answer B
```
Subject: Cracked tiles in bathroom — reported by phone on Thursday [date]

Hello [Contractor name],

I am putting in writing what I reported to you by telephone on Thursday [date], since you have since told me that no report was made.

On Thursday [date] I phoned you and told you that the new tiles in the bathroom at [address] are cracked. That was the substance of the call: the tiles are cracked, and I notified you of it on that date.

The tiles remain cracked and the defect is unresolved. Please confirm in writing that you have received this notice, and let me know when you will inspect the work and how you intend to put it right. I am available on [days/times].

I am keeping this email as a record of the report and of its date.

Regards,
[Your name]
[Phone number]
```

Fill in the bracketed items — I have not invented the date, address or phone number. Send it tonight: the value of this email is the timestamp on a disputed date, and every hour you hold it is an hour he can point to; a Sunday 10:30pm send costs you nothing but looks exactly like what it is, a record being made.
