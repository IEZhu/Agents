**How to tell him**

- Do it in person, somewhere private, at a time when he isn't in the middle of a crisis. You'll probably want a way to leave afterward, such as your own place or a friend you can go to.
- Keep it short and about you, not a case against him. For example: *"I've thought about this a lot, and I've decided to end our relationship. I'm not happy, and I don't see that changing. This isn't open for negotiation. I wanted to tell you face to face."*
- Don't list his faults or mention narcissism. A list of accusations invites him to argue each point, and then you're debating instead of leaving.
- If he asks "why?", give one honest sentence, then repeat your decision: *"I've felt unseen for a while, and I've decided. I'm not going to go back and forth on it."*

**How to not get sucked back in**

- **Write down your reasons today, while you're clear.** When he's upset or kind, or when you feel lonely, reread the list instead of relying on how you feel in the moment.
- **Expect some guilt, and don't treat it as a signal.** Missing him or feeling bad for hurting him is normal after three years. It doesn't mean you made the wrong call.
- **Treat his reaction as data, not a verdict.** If he cries, pleads, or promises to change, that tells you he's upset. It doesn't create a new obligation for you.
- **Decide your contact rules ahead of time.** For example: no late-night calls, logistics by text only, and no meeting up "just to talk" for at least a month. Rules you set in advance are much easier to keep than choices you make in the moment.
