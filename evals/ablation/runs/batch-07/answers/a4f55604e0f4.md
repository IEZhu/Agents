**Subject: Approval request: move our search cluster to a managed AWS service**

**The ask**
Approve moving our search infrastructure from servers we run ourselves to Amazon's managed service (Amazon OpenSearch Service). Engineering is asking for sign-off by Friday.

**Why now**
Our current search software (Elasticsearch 7.10) is stuck on an old version because of a vendor licensing change, and it no longer receives security patches. Staying on it means a growing security risk. Upgrading instead would require a new license, which would need legal and cost review and hasn't been priced.

**Financial impact**

| | Today | After migration |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly infrastructure cost | ~$14.2k | ~$11.8k |
| Ongoing engineering upkeep | ~0.5 FTE (patching, upgrades) | Mostly handled by AWS |

- **Run-rate savings:** ~$2.4k/month, about **$29k/year** in infrastructure alone.
- **Freed capacity:** about half of one engineer's time moves from maintenance to other work.
- **One-time cost:** about 6 engineer-weeks of effort. We also pay for both systems during a 2-week overlap period.

**Risks (all rated manageable by engineering)**
- Some internal dashboards need to be rebuilt in the new tool.
- Two custom components aren't supported and will be replaced with standard features.
- Searches on data older than 30 days may be 10–15% slower. That data moves to cheaper storage, and this is where much of the savings comes from.
- The final switchover happens during a scheduled maintenance window.

**Recommendation**
Approve. The move lowers monthly cost, removes an unpatched security exposure, and avoids an open-ended license negotiation.
