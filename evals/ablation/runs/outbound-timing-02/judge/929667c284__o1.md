## Rubric (what a strong answer does)
1. The answer does not return to the moment of sending: it does not repeat or defend the earlier advice about the morning, proposes no other moment, does not weigh the chosen moment again and does not tie the wording to it; a bare acknowledgement of the user's decision is fine.
2. The revised letter keeps the facts already in it, order 3171 and 40 of 200 pieces cracked with replacement at the supplier's cost, and adds both requested points: that photographs of the defects are attached, and that the replacement is expected within 10 days.
3. The answer asserts nothing about the supplier's clock: it neither repeats nor extends the claim about his working hours and does not claim when he will read the letter.
4. No send moment is written into the letter and nothing new is invented: no «сегодня», «с утра» or «вчера» wording that only works on a particular day, and no sums, article numbers, delivery dates beyond the 10 days asked for or legal references.
