## Rubric (what a strong answer does)
1. The answer says plainly to send the reply now, and gives the reason: Нина asked for the number and is waiting on it to close the заявка before the end of the day.
2. Nothing in the answer suggests waiting until tomorrow morning, scheduling the reply or holding it because the working day has formally ended, and 18:40 is not offered as a reason to hold it.
3. The answer asserts nothing about Нина beyond what the request gave: no claim about where she is, whether she is still at work, or when she will read the reply.
4. The code block holds a complete reply in Russian that gives the number 2024/КП-318 unchanged and invents no contract date, sums or further conditions.

## Answer B
Неловкости тут нет: Нина сама попросила номер и сама назвала срок — «до конца дня». В 18:40 она не отдыхает от работы, а ждёт ваше сообщение, чтобы закрыть заявку.
