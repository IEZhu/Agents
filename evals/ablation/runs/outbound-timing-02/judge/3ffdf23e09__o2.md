## Latest user message
Which of these two subject lines tells customers what is changing before they open the email? Answer in one sentence.
A: Important update regarding your subscription
B: Your plan goes from $9 to $12 on 1 February

## Rubric (what a strong answer does)
1. The answer is one sentence and picks B.
2. It gives the reason: B names the new price and the date it takes effect, while A does not say what the update is.
3. The answer gives no advice or remark about when to send the email (time of day, day of the week, waiting or sending now).
4. It does not rewrite either subject line and does not offer a third option that was not asked for.

## Answer A
B — it names the change, the amounts and the date in the subject itself, while A only signals that something happened and forces the open to find out what.


## Answer B
B tells them what is changing — the price moving from $9 to $12 and the date it takes effect — while A only signals that something about the subscription has changed and forces them to open the email to find out what.
