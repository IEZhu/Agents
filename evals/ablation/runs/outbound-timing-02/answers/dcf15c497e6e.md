```
Subject: Notice of Claim — Water Damage from Ceiling Leak, Policy [POLICY NUMBER]

Dear [Insurer / Claims Department],

I am giving written notice of a claim under my homeowners policy, number [POLICY NUMBER], for the property at [PROPERTY ADDRESS].

On [MONTH] 3, [YEAR], I discovered a water leak from the ceiling in the [ROOM]. The affected areas are [LIST: e.g. ceiling, drywall, flooring, and contents]. I have taken photographs of the damage and have [taken / not yet taken] steps to prevent further loss, including [e.g. shutting off the water supply, placing containers, running a dehumidifier].

I have not yet had the cause of the leak professionally assessed and cannot state the full extent of the damage at this time. I will provide repair estimates, invoices, photographs, and any other documentation you require.

Please confirm receipt of this notice, the claim number assigned, and the next steps, including whether an adjuster will inspect the property.

My contact details are [PHONE] and [EMAIL].

Sincerely,
[FULL NAME]
[PROPERTY ADDRESS]
[DATE]
```

Fill in the bracketed fields and send it tonight: today is the last day of the notice window, so sending it tomorrow puts the notice outside it — and a short notice filed on time beats a complete one filed late, since you can add the assessment and estimates afterward.
